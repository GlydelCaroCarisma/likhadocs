CHAPTER I - INTRODUCTION (Page 1)

1.1 Background of the Study

In today’s generation, local fashion makers and artisans contribute significantly to the preservation of Filipino culture through their handmade products. However, many of them still rely on traditional selling methods such as word-of-mouth, small community events, and social media platforms. While these methods provide some level of exposure, they are often limited and lack a structured system for managing product listings, orders, and customer interactions. As digital commerce continues to expand, the absence of a dedicated and organized online platform puts local makers at a disadvantage.

One of the major challenges faced by local artisans is their limited access to wider markets and effective online selling tools. Fast fashion brands dominate digital platforms, making it difficult for small-scale makers to compete and gain visibility. As a result, their products often go unnoticed, leading to lower income and fewer growth opportunities. At the same time, buyers who are interested in unique, locally made products struggle to discover and purchase these items due to the lack of a centralized and accessible marketplace.

To address these issues, Likha.ph: A Cloud-Integrated Digital Marketplace for Local Makers is proposed. The system aims to provide a dedicated platform where artisans can register, showcase, and sell their products directly to buyers in a more efficient and organized manner. By integrating cloud-based technology and web services, the platform ensures secure transactions, reliable data management, and an improved user experience. This study seeks to empower local makers by expanding their market reach, increasing their income opportunities, and promoting Filipino craftsmanship in the digital marketplace.

1.2 Problem Statement and Objectives

Problem Statement

  1. How can a web-based platform be developed to help local fashion makers register, manage their profiles, and sell their handmade products online?
  2. How can the system provide buyers with an easy and efficient way to browse, search, and purchase locally made fashion products?
  3. How can cloud technology and web services be utilized to improve system performance, data management, and accessibility?
