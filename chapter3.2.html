CHAPTER III - METHODOLOGY AND SYSTEM DEVELOPMENT (Page 2)

Deployment Phase

After successful testing, the system was deployed to a live server, making it accessible to users. This phase involves setting up the hosting environment and ensuring that all features are fully operational online.

Maintenance Phase

The system is continuously monitored and maintained to ensure optimal performance. Updates, bug fixes, and feature enhancements are implemented regularly based on user feedback and system performance.

3.2 Requirement Gathering and Analysis

Data Gathering Methods

Interview. Interviews were conducted with local makers, artisans, and potential buyers to understand their needs, challenges, and expectations in selling and purchasing handmade products online.

Survey. A survey questionnaire was distributed to gather quantitative data regarding user preferences, online shopping behavior, and desired features in a digital marketplace.

Observation. Existing practices in selling handmade and local products, such as the use of social media platforms and manual transaction processes, were observed.

Functional Requirements

  • The system shall allow users to register and create accounts as buyers or makers.
  • The system shall allow makers to post products for sale.
  • The system shall enable buyers to browse available products.
  • The system shall allow buyers to submit purchase requests or add items to a cart.
  • The system shall provide administrative management of users, products, and transactions.
  • The system shall process online payments securely.

Non-Functional Requirements

Performance: The system must respond to user requests within an acceptable time and support multiple simultaneous users browsing products or making transactions.
