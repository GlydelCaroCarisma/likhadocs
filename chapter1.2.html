CHAPTER I - INTRODUCTION (Page 2)

Specific Objectives

  1. To design and develop a web-based platform that allows local fashion makers to register accounts, manage profiles, and list products online.
  2. To develop buyer-side features that allow users to browse, search, and purchase locally made fashion products through an easy-to-use interface.
  3. To implement web services that support efficient communication between system modules such as user management, product management, and order processing.
  4. To support local fashion makers in expanding their market reach by providing an online selling platform that reduces dependence on traditional selling methods.
  5. To integrate cloud technology for secure storage of user, product, and transaction data, enabling real-time system access and improved availability.

1.3 Scope, Delimitation, and Significance

Scope

  • The system will be a cloud-integrated web-based digital marketplace for local fashion makers.
  • It will include features such as user registration and login, profile management, product listing, product browsing and searching, and order processing.
  • It supports the following users: local fashion makers (sellers), buyers (customers), and system administrators.

Delimitation

  • Limited to local fashion products only and accessible through web browsers with internet connection.
  • The system will not include mobile application development.

Significance of the Study

  • Users – It provides buyers with a convenient and user-friendly platform to browse, search, and purchase unique, locally made fashion products online. It also helps sellers (local fashion makers) easily showcase and sell their products to a wider market.
  • Organization – It supports local fashion communities and small businesses by increasing their visibility, improving sales opportunities, and promoting Filipino craftsmanship through a centralized digital marketplace.
  • Developers – It can enhance their skills in system design, development, and implementation.
  • Future Researchers – It can serve as a reference for future research in the area of e-commerce systems, digital markets, and cloud computing platforms, which can be improved or expanded with new features and technology.
